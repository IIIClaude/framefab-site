Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short

FrameFab does not collect your data. Your projects, clients, prices, profile systems, offcuts, settings and logo are stored only on your phone. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or your work to us.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter: client names and phone numbers, addresses, window sizes, prices, your business details and logo. We never receive it. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it.

A logo you pick is read from your photos only when you choose it, and only that one picture is kept, inside the app. Quote PDFs, cut plan PDFs and backup files are created on your phone and go only where you send them with the share sheet (for example WhatsApp, e-mail or your files).

Purchases

FrameFab Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your name, e-mail or card. To check whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier made by the app (not linked to your name or contact details), your purchase records from the store, and basic device information such as the app version, operating system version and country of the store. This is the only network connection the app makes. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Apple's and Google's.

Children

FrameFab is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. We will not start collecting your data without saying so here first.
